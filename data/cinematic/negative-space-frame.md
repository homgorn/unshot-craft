Cinematic frame of [Subject] small and placed low-right, the upper two thirds left
as clean empty wall, even soft light, no clutter, muted palette, 35mm, ample
headroom for a headline.

## Lede

A frame with real empty space is a layout that has already reserved its own copy
area. Photographing with the composition you intend to typeset into is the
difference between a design that feels designed and one that feels assembled.

## When to use

Landing page heroes, social cards, and any shot that will carry a headline. Pair it
with the motion card that later demotes the title into a section label.

## Directing the AI

Give a proportion, not a vibe: "upper two thirds empty wall". "Lots of negative
space" produces an empty frame with the subject dead centre.

## Common mistakes

- Empty space that is not clean, so a headline lands on texture.
- Subject placed on the third line but cropped by the frame edge.
- Light falloff in the copy area, which makes text unreadable over it.
