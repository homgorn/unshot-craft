Documentary-style beat of [Subject] observed in use with no presenter, no voiceover,
natural ambient light only, long unbroken take, hands and screen in frame, 35mm,
shallow focus, observational framing.

## Lede

Showing the tool in someone's hands, unscripted, borrows credibility that a presenter
cannot manufacture. The absence of explanation is the technique: the viewer infers
the use case, and an inference is more persuasive than a claim.

## When to use

B2B and developer audiences, long-form landing pages, and anywhere a polished
presenter shot would read as an ad.

## Directing the AI

"No presenter, no voiceover, ambient light only" must all be said. The model
defaults to a polished studio look with a talking head, which is the exact opposite
of what is being asked.

## Common mistakes

- Drifting into a staged three-quarter hero angle mid-take.
- Adding ambient music cues that signal "commercial".
- Over-stabilising, which makes a handheld take look like a tripod.
