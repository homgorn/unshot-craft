## Lede

A palette does work when every colour in it has the same temperature. The cold blue
look survives scrutiny because nothing in the frame is warm enough to break it —
the failure mode is one skin-tone or one warm prop in a supposedly cool scene.

## When to use

Infrastructure and developer tooling, hardware, and enterprise work where "serious"
is the register being aimed at.

## Directing the AI

State the temperature constraint as a prohibition as well as a direction: "no warm
tones". A palette described only positively will still admit a warm accent.

## Common mistakes

- One warm element surviving in the background.
- Over-saturating the blue until it reads as a filter rather than a material.
- Losing micro-contrast, which turns "precise" into "soft".
