Documentary shot of [Subject] in its real environment, available window light only,
handheld but composed, muted natural colour, no stylisation, slight exposure
irregularity, 35mm, observational distance.

## Lede

Naturalism is a constraint list: existing light, real locations, no styling, no
clean-up. What it buys is belief. A viewer who suspects a set stops evaluating the
content and starts evaluating the production.

## When to use

Testimonials, case studies, and anything where credibility matters more than polish.

## Directing the AI

"Available window light only, no stylisation, no colour grading" is the set of
clauses. Leave any of them out and the model applies its default commercial-grade
finish, which is exactly what the genre is avoiding.

## Common mistakes

- Styling the environment, which reads as a set.
- Perfect exposure, which reads as a still rather than a frame from a moving shot.
- Over-clean skin and walls, which is the fastest way to lose naturalism.
