Two-beat product story: the first frame shows [Subject] mid-friction, the second
frame shows the same subject resolved and calm, matched framing and lighting across
both beats so only the state changes, 50mm, no text.

## Lede

The most durable product structure is a state change, not a feature list. Two frames,
matched everything except the thing that is now solved. Because the camera language
does not change, the viewer reads the difference as a change in the world rather
than a change in the edit.

## When to use

Launch films, paid social, and anywhere a viewer has about eight seconds of
attention. It is also the structure the upstream 36.2s template is built on.

## Directing the AI

Ask for the matched pair explicitly: "identical framing and lighting in both beats".
Without that clause you get two different shots, and the comparison dies.

## Common mistakes

- Different lighting between beats, which makes it read as two products.
- Solving the problem with a hard cut to a logo instead of to the product.
- Adding on-screen text, which competes with the state change.
