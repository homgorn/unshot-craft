Retro 1970s comedy scene with [Subject] shot on vintage 16mm, slightly soft focus,
visible gate weave, warm amber cast, wide static framing with the subject small in
frame, deadpan timing, no camera movement.

## Lede

Genre framing is mostly about how much frame you leave empty and how still the
camera is. A 70s comedy shot is soft, warm, static and wide, and the stillness is
what leaves room for the timing to do the work.

## When to use

Humour, explainers that want warmth, and any brand that benefits from looking
handmade rather than clinical.

## Directing the AI

Name the stock and the framing: "vintage 16mm, warm amber, wide static framing,
subject small in frame". "Retro" alone produces a costume-drama look, not a comedy
one.

## Common mistakes

- Adding camera movement, which kills the deadpan timing the genre depends on.
- Pushing the subject too large, which loses the dead air the jokes land in.
- Over-sharpening, which reads as "old filter" instead of old film.
