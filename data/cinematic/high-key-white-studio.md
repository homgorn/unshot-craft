High-key studio product photograph of [Subject] on a seamless white sweep, soft
overhead key with a fill card, shadow falling softly beneath the object, colour
accurate, even exposure with no clipping, 100mm macro, commercial catalogue look.

## Lede

High-key is an exposure discipline: keep every surface inside the range where the
material is still readable. Clip the white background and the object stops looking
like an object and starts looking like a shape cut out of paper.

## When to use

Catalogue and e-commerce, marketplaces, and any frame where the viewer needs to
judge the actual colour and material of the product.

## Directing the AI

"Even exposure, no clipping, colour accurate" plus "soft shadow beneath". Without
the clipping clause, white backgrounds blow out and take the product edge with them.

## Common mistakes

- Clipped highlights on a white object against white, which erases its silhouette.
- Hard multiple shadows from an unstated multi-light setup.
- Colour shifts from aggressive white balance.
