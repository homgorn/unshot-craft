# Shot recipe cards

157 cards across 10 categories, 218 motion styles, 1057 documented parameters, 792 pitfalls. Every row links to a per-card page.

## How to choose a card

1. Pick the narrative beat (open / feature / transition / close) — that is the category.
2. Read the energy ladder: the film opens low, alternates mid-high with breath beats, peaks at the outro (`references/sequences/promo-energy-arc.md`).
3. Check `duration_frames`: a card longer than ~150f needs a breath beat after it.
4. Read the card's known pitfalls before wiring it in — the budget rules (≤2 crash zooms, ≤2 light effects per film) are hard limits, not suggestions.
5. Copy the referenced demo TSX rather than rewriting it: the tuned easing and timing ratios in the demo are the truth; the card only states the intent.
